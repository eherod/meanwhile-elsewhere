WELCOME


In November 2026, I came across a video on YouTube that discussed the Web Revival and the IndieWeb. I realized that for nearly two decades, I had completely forgotten about the way I used the internet in the late '90s and early 2000s. Sometime around 2003, I discovered Myspace, and this became my primary digital home until 2007, when I left for Facebook. By 2010, YouTube began consuming the majority of my time on the internet, and until recently, I hadn't ventured far from there.


Inspired by the Web Revival, this website is my return to the internet. Like most places out here, you should expect to find it continuously under construction. If you are stumbling in here in late 2026, please know that I have only recently built the structure and there is still much work to do.


In addition to serving as a repository for my personal writings and projects, the primary aim of this site is to curate links to websites that I find interesting and useful. I hope it will serve as a valuable waypoint for others like me who are turning away from the corporate web in search of a more organic and authentic online experience.
